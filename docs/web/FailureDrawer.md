# File: `web/src/components/FailureDrawer.tsx`

**What it is:** The drill-down panel for one failure: the AI's reasoning and
signals, the error and stack trace, history stats, the Jira match, every
re-run attempt with its error, and all attachments (screenshots, logs).

**Why it exists:** "Don't just tell me — show me the evidence." This is where
a skeptical stakeholder or QA engineer verifies any claim.
